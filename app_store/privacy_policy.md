# Privacy Policy for walkaround

**Last Updated: August 21, 2026**

walkaround ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage in our iOS mobile application.

## 1. Zero Cloud Tracking & On-Device Storage
walkaround is engineered as an **offline-first, privacy-by-design application**:
- All vehicle inspection photographs, damage notes, and dispute dossiers are stored **strictly locally on your device**.
- We do not operate user accounts, and we do not maintain a central database of your photos or travel destinations.
- Your data is never sold, rented, or monetized for advertising purposes.

## 2. Permissions & Data Access
walkaround requests the following device permissions solely to deliver essential features:
- **Camera Access:** Used exclusively while using the app to capture 360-degree vehicle inspection photographs and dashboard readings.
- **Location Services (When in Use):** Used to obtain geographic coordinates and reverse-geocode your pickup/dropoff facility (e.g. airport rental lot). This data is burned into the forensic metadata HUD overlay directly on your device.
- **Photo Library Access:** Used only when you choose to export generated PDF dispute packages or save comparison photos to your device storage.

## 3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions
Financial transactions (Single Trip Pass, Annual Pro, Lifetime) are processed securely by Apple via StoreKit and the App Store. walkaround never receives, stores, or accesses your credit card or payment information.

## 4. Children's Privacy
walkaround is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## 5. Contact Us
For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact support at: `privacy@walkaround.app`.
